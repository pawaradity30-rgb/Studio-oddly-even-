Our Story

Where odd
meets even

Studio Oddly Even was founded on the belief that the finest spaces emerge from the tension between opposite forces — bold and restrained, ancient and contemporary, raw and refined.

Led by Tejal Shrotriya and Nirbhay Shah, our boutique practice operates at the intersection of architecture, interiors, and preservation — creating spaces that speak both to the present and to history.

Tejal Shrotriya
Principal Architect
B.Arch, PVP College Pune. Masters in Historic Preservation, Cornell University. Industry experience in Manhattan, New York.
Nirbhay Shah
Design Director
Specializing in spatial design, interior landscapes, and the art of transforming constrained spaces into extraordinary environments.

What we
create
